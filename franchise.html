Process Flow

From First Enquiry to Open Campus

Nine stages, each with a clear decision point. Nothing is committed until both sides are satisfied.

A

Enquiry

You find us through this website, an advertisement, word of mouth, or a visit to our Prof. Waris Mir Campus, and register your interest.

B

Introductory Presentation

A relationship manager walks you through the academic model, the AI and Robotics programme, the support structure, and the commercial terms.

C

Site and Vicinity Inspection

Our quality assurance team visits your proposed building and surveys the surrounding catchment, including the other schools already serving it.

D

Memorandum of Understanding

Once the assessment is satisfactory, both parties sign an MOU setting out intent, territory, and the campus type to be established.

E

Post-MOU Consultancy Brief

We hand over the setup brief: classroom and lab layout, equipment schedule, staffing plan, academic calendar, and launch timeline.

F

Second Facility Visit

Quality assurance returns to confirm the building has been prepared to the standard set out in the brief before anything is finalised.

G

Franchise Fee Settlement

On approval, the balance of the franchise fee is settled. Commercial details are shared privately with each applicant.

H

Franchise Agreement Signed

The formal agreement is executed, confirming your territory, campus scope, brand rights, and academic obligations.

I

You Become a Network Partner

Onboarding begins: teacher training, brand and marketing rollout, admissions setup, and support through to your first day of classes.

Your proposed property is assessed on the building itself and on what the surrounding area can support.

Common Questions

Franchise FAQs

Can I convert my existing school into a London International School campus?

Yes. Conversions of established schools are welcome alongside entirely new campuses. Our team reviews your current enrolment, building, and staffing, then sets out what would change under our academic system.

Which cities are open?

Enquiries are open across Pakistan. Our flagship Prof. Waris Mir Campus is in Township, Lahore. You may nominate up to three preferred cities and three preferred areas within them, and we will assess catchment and territory availability.

What investment is required?

It depends on the campus type, the size of the building, and whether you are converting an existing school or opening a new one. Share your plans on WhatsApp or through the enquiry form and we will send the commercial details relevant to your case.

What building do I need?

The property may be owned, rented, or still to be arranged, and either residential or commercial. Our quality assurance team inspects the building and its vicinity before any agreement is signed.

How long does the process take?

The process runs through nine stages, from first enquiry to onboarding as a network partner. Timelines depend largely on how quickly the building and local approvals are ready.

Do I need a background in education?

It helps, but it is not essential. Many partners come from business rather than teaching. What matters is the commitment to the academic standard and the willingness to let our team train and support your staff.
