Admissions Open 2026-27

School Admission in Lahore for 2026-27

Admissions are open at London International School — Prof. Waris Mir Campus, a Cambridge school in Township, Lahore. We welcome children from Pre-Nursery through Class 7, with AI and robotics from age 3. Here is how to apply, and how to book a campus tour before you decide.

Book a Campus Tour Apply on WhatsApp

London International School — Prof. Waris Mir Campus is a Cambridge Pathway Registered private school in Township, Lahore, with admissions open for the 2026-27 session. The school accepts children from Pre-Nursery through Class 7, roughly ages 3 to 12, with Class 8 launching in 2026-27. Admissions run on a rolling basis until each class is full, so families are encouraged to apply early. The campus is on Ali Road opposite Ideal Park, serving Township, Johar Town, Model Town, and Garden Town. To apply, families book a campus tour, submit an application, complete a short informal assessment, and confirm enrolment. The admissions team can be reached on WhatsApp at 0301-0499777.

How to Apply

Five Steps to Enrolment

The whole process is designed to be calm and unhurried, so you can decide with confidence.

  • 1

    Book a campus tour

    Message us on WhatsApp at 0301-0499777, or use the admissions page. We book tours Monday to Saturday, 8 AM to 4 PM.

  • 2

    Visit and meet the team

    Walk through the campus, meet the class teachers, and see the children at work. You meet the people who will teach your child, not just the registrar.

  • 3

    Submit the application

    Complete the application form for your child's class and submit the required documents. The admissions team tells you exactly what to bring.

  • 4

    Informal assessment

    Your child takes part in a short, age-appropriate assessment. It is relaxed and low-pressure, and it simply helps us place your child in the right class.

  • 5

    Confirm enrolment

    Once a place is offered, confirm enrolment. The admissions team will walk you through the fee structure and your child's start date.

Why Families Apply

More Than a Local School

Parents searching for a school near them in south-west Lahore have plenty of options. What sets London International School apart is not just location, though our Township campus is a short drive from Johar Town, Model Town, and Garden Town. It is what happens inside the classroom.

We are a Cambridge school in Lahore where AI and robotics run through every year from age 3. Children earn two US coding certifications by Kindergarten, work with LEGO Mindstorms and Arduino as they grow, and reach 3D printing and AI projects by Class 7. In-house emotional-health counsellors support every child, and regular parent-teacher updates keep you close to how your child is settling. The school is named in honour of Prof. Waris Mir, the academic and journalist, father of Hamid Mir.

If you are comparing schools for 2026-27, the fastest way to decide is to visit. Book a tour and see a Cambridge classroom, and the robotics lab, for yourself.

Frequently Asked Questions

Admissions in Lahore, 2026-27

Are school admissions in Lahore open for 2026-27?

Yes. Admissions for the 2026-27 session are open at London International School in Township, Lahore, for Pre-Nursery through Class 7. Class 8 launches in 2026-27. Places are limited, so early applications are encouraged.

What is the last date for school admission in 2026?

There is no single fixed cut-off. Admissions run on a rolling basis until each class is full, so the practical deadline depends on how quickly a class fills. To check current availability for your child's class, message the admissions team on WhatsApp at 0301-0499777.

How do I apply for admission?

Book a campus tour on WhatsApp at 0301-0499777 or through the admissions page. The steps are: tour the campus, submit the application and documents, a short informal assessment for your child, and then confirm enrolment.

Which classes are accepting admissions?

Pre-Nursery, Nursery, Kindergarten, and Classes 1 to 7, covering roughly ages 3 to 12. Class 8 opens in 2026-27. We do not currently offer IGCSE, O-Level, or Class 9 and above.

Do you accept in-year or mid-session admissions?

Where a class has space, we can consider in-year admissions so your child does not have to wait for the next session. Availability changes through the year, so message the admissions team on WhatsApp at 0301-0499777 to check your child's class.

What are the admission fees?

Fees depend on your child's class. Message the admissions team on WhatsApp at 0301-0499777 with your child's class, and we will send the current fee structure, the enrolment timeline, and any available discounts.
