## 5. Location-Specific Content (Local On-Page SEO)

**Lahore signal strength:**
- "Lahore" appears in `<title>` tags on: `about.html`, `academics.html`, `campus.html` (confirmed from meta checks), `ask-prof-mir.html`.
- `index.html` title does NOT contain "Lahore" — the homepage title is `"London School — Pakistan's Most Advanced AI & Robotics School With Cambridge Pathway"`. This is a missed opportunity. A query like "best Cambridge school in Lahore" will not find a direct title-match signal on the most authoritative page.
- Meta description on `index.html` ends with "Lahore." — present but trailing.
- Schema `areaServed` and `addressLocality` both set to Lahore.
- Landmark references (Ali Road, Ideal Park Township, Township) appear in footer across all pages, contact section, FAQ, and `yearbook.html` prose — strong natural-language signal.
- Blog content: 4 blog posts, 2 explicitly targeting Lahore queries (`choosing-cambridge-school-lahore.html`, `lahore-school-holidays-2026.html`) — good local content foundation.

**Catchment area coverage:**
No neighbourhood-specific content beyond "Township" and "Ali Road". Queries from parents in Johar Town, Iqbal Town, Faisal Town, or Gulberg will not find neighbourhood-specific signals. A short "Serving families across Township, Johar Town, and Iqbal Town, Lahore" line in the contact section or footer would improve coverage without creating new pages.

**Cambridge Pathway accreditation:**
Prominently displayed — proof bar, meta titles, schema `memberOf`, banner rotator, and multiple body copy mentions. This is the strongest verifiable trust signal and is well-used.

**Student count:**
Schema `description` references "Established 2025" but does not include student count. The proof bar shows "200+ Students Enrolled" — good local social proof. The schema's `description` could include this.

## Proximity Note

Proximity accounts for approximately 55.2% of local pack ranking variance (Search Atlas ML study, 2025). This is determined by the searcher's physical location relative to the school — it cannot be influenced by on-page or schema changes. The school's position in Township, Lahore will naturally surface it for queries from that catchment. For queries from Johar Town or Gulberg (1.5–3km), the fixes above (title, schema, neighbourhood copy) will partially compensate.
