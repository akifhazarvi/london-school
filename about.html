Built on the Legacy of Prof. Waris Mir

Named in honour of the legendary academic, writer, and journalist, father of renowned journalist Hamid Mir. A warm, nurturing Cambridge Pathway Registered school in Lahore where children explore AI, build robots, play sports, and discover what they love.

Welcome

Message from the Principal

Welcome to London School, Pakistan's premier AI and robotics school. We believe that in a world transformed by technology, every child deserves to be a builder, not just a consumer. AI and robotics aren't add-ons to our curriculum; they're foundational. Every subject, from language to science, is infused with hands-on, real-world problem-solving.

Here, your child becomes confident in technology while remaining deeply human. We develop critical thinkers, compassionate leaders, and curious builders. Small class sizes, teachers who truly know each student, and learning environments where mistakes fuel discovery: that's how we nurture the next generation of innovators.

We'd love to meet you and your child. Come experience the London School difference firsthand.

Our Hiring Philosophy

What we look for in a London School teacher

A school is only as strong as the people in front of its children. This is the bar every teacher we hire must clear, and the reason our parents stop us in the corridor to thank us by name.

1. A relevant qualification, plus the willingness to keep learning

Every classroom teacher at London School holds a Bachelor's or Master's qualification in education, the subject they teach, or early-childhood development. That is the floor. The ceiling is what they do after they are hired: every term, every teacher attends Academic Plan sessions and Cambridge-aligned curriculum workshops. We will hire a less-experienced teacher who is hungry to grow over a more-experienced teacher who has stopped.

2. The ability to teach a child, not just a class

Our class sizes are deliberately small so a teacher can know each child by name, by strength, and by struggle. In every interview we ask: tell us about a child you couldn't reach, and what you tried. Teachers who answer with a story remember they are working with children, not curricula. Teachers who deflect to "all children are different" do not move forward.

3. Comfort with technology, without being defined by it

Because AI and robotics start at age 3 here, every teacher must be comfortable using digital tools alongside whiteboards, books, and outdoor play. We do not need every teacher to be a coder. We need every teacher to look at a tablet and ask "is this the best way to teach this idea right now?" rather than reach for it by default.

4. Warmth that matches our children

Children remember how a teacher made them feel a long time after they have forgotten what the teacher said. We hire for warmth, patience, and the ability to greet a three-year-old at the door without making them anxious. This is non-negotiable, and it is the single most common reason a candidate with strong credentials does not get an offer.

5. A mind that is genuinely curious

If we want our students to ask great questions, the adults teaching them have to be people who still ask great questions themselves. We ask candidates: what are you currently learning, outside school? The answer tells us almost everything we need to know.

Every teacher you meet on a campus tour was hired against this five-part bar. If you would like to meet a few of them in person, book a visit.
