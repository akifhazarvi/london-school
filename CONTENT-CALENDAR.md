## Content Quality Standards

Every post must:
- Be **at least 1,000 words** for guides / 600 words for news/event posts
- Include a **byline** (founder, principal, or named teacher) — never "Admin" or "Team"
- Cite at least one **external authoritative source** (Cambridge Assessment, government education stats, etc.) when making claims
- Have **3+ original photos** from campus or events — no stock imagery for editorial posts
- Include a **clear CTA** to `/admissions/visit/` or `/contact/`
- Use **brochure language** for any branded claims (no invented statistics)

## E-E-A-T Building Plan

- **Experience:** First-person teacher/principal perspectives in posts. Photos of *our* lab, *our* pool, *our* classrooms.
- **Expertise:** Faculty bios with degrees, certifications, training. Cambridge credentials clearly displayed.
- **Authoritativeness:** Hamid Mir / Waris Mir legacy framing where relevant. Cambridge Pathway and LAPS affiliation badges. Press mentions when secured.
- **Trustworthiness:** Transparent fees (matched to brochure), real photos, named principal, working phone numbers, accurate hours, GBP reviews.
