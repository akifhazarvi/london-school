Find Our Campus

A Cambridge School on Ali Road, Opposite Ideal Park

London School — Prof. Waris Mir Campus is in Sector B-2 of Township, Lahore. We are a short drive from Johar Town, Model Town, Iqbal Town, and Garden Town, and we welcome family visits Monday to Saturday.

London School — Prof. Waris Mir Campus is a Cambridge Pathway Registered private school in Lahore, Pakistan. The campus is located at Plot #8, Sector B-2, Block 1, Ali Road, directly opposite Ideal Park Township. Township is one of Lahore's most established residential neighbourhoods, sitting between Model Town and Johar Town in the south-west of the city, separated from Johar Town by College Road and roughly four kilometres from Ferozepur Road. The school serves families across Township, Johar Town, Iqbal Town, Allama Iqbal Town, Model Town, and Garden Town, and offers Cambridge primary education from Pre-Nursery through Class 7, with Class 8 launching in 2026–27.

Areas We Serve

Families Travel from Across South-West Lahore

Township sits in the heart of south-west Lahore, with quick access to several of the city's most established residential neighbourhoods.

Township, Lahore

Our home neighbourhood. The campus is in Sector B-2 of Township, on Ali Road. Township parents typically reach the school in a short drive through the local road network.

Johar Town

Johar Town is directly across College Road from Township. Families typically take College Road into Township, then turn onto Ali Road. Many Johar Town parents are within a 10-minute drive.

Model Town

Model Town is one of Lahore's most established neighbourhoods and sits adjacent to Township. Families head into Township via the local road network and follow signs for Ali Road.

Iqbal Town and Allama Iqbal Town

Allama Iqbal Town parents reach us by heading south through the Multan Road and Wahdat Road network into Township. We have several families who travel from Iqbal Town daily.

Garden Town

Garden Town is well-connected to Township via the Ferozepur Road and Wahdat Road corridors. The campus is roughly four kilometres from Ferozepur Road.

Other Areas

Local Landmarks

What's Around the Campus

If you are new to Township, these are the landmarks parents use to find us.

  • 1

    Ideal Park Township

    Our most direct landmark. The school is on Ali Road, directly opposite the entrance to Ideal Park. If you can find Ideal Park, you have found us.

  • 2

    College Road

    College Road forms the boundary between Township and Johar Town. It is one of the busiest roads in this part of Lahore. From College Road, turn into Township and follow local signs to Ali Road.

  • 3

    Madina Market

    Madina Market is the central commercial hub of Township and a well-known reference point for anyone giving directions inside the neighbourhood.

  • 4

    Khokhar Chowk and Mochipura Mor

    Both are commonly used navigation points along the College Road and Maulana Shaukat Ali Road corridors. If your driver knows either, they can find Ali Road from there.

  • 5

    Ferozepur Road

    The campus is approximately four kilometres from Ferozepur Road, one of Lahore's main north-south arteries. Families coming from Ferozepur Road exit onto the Township road network and follow signs for Ali Road.

Why This Location

A Quiet, Established Neighbourhood for Children

Township is one of Lahore's oldest planned residential schemes. The neighbourhood is dense with established families, mature trees, neighbourhood mosques and markets, and a long tradition of schools. That last point matters. Township is home to several of Lahore's well-known schools, which means the area is built for the rhythm of a school morning: drop-off lanes, calm side streets, and parents who know where to slow down.

For a young child starting their first years of formal education, location matters more than parents sometimes realise. A short, predictable commute means an unhurried morning. Familiar landmarks help young children feel oriented and safe. And being inside an established neighbourhood, rather than at the edge of one, means the school is part of a community parents can drop into for a quick visit, a lost-jumper recovery, or an unscheduled chat with a teacher.

This is why we chose Township for Prof. Waris Mir Campus. Children deserve a school they can reach without a 45-minute drive across Lahore, and parents deserve a campus that fits naturally into the rest of their day.

Frequently Asked Questions

Visiting and Finding Us

Where exactly is London School in Lahore?

London School — Prof. Waris Mir Campus is at Plot #8, Sector B-2, Block 1, Ali Road, Opposite Ideal Park Township, Lahore 54600. The campus is in Sector B-2 of Township, with Ideal Park directly across the road as the main landmark.

Which Lahore neighbourhoods do you serve?

Our families come from across south-west Lahore, including Township, Johar Town, Iqbal Town, Allama Iqbal Town, Model Town, and Garden Town. Township sits between Model Town and Johar Town, with College Road forming the boundary between Township and Johar Town.

How do I get to the campus from Johar Town?

Johar Town is directly across College Road from Township. Take College Road into Township, then turn onto Ali Road. The school is on Ali Road, opposite the entrance to Ideal Park. Most Johar Town families reach the campus in a short drive depending on traffic.

How do I get to the campus from Model Town?

Township is adjacent to Model Town in south-west Lahore. From Model Town, head into Township via the main road network and follow signs for College Road or Ali Road. The school is on Ali Road, directly opposite Ideal Park Township.

What landmarks are near the school?

The most direct landmark is Ideal Park Township, which is opposite the school on Ali Road. Other nearby Township landmarks include Madina Market (Township's central commercial hub), Ashiq Market on College Road, Khokhar Chowk, and Mochipura Mor. The campus is roughly four kilometres from Ferozepur Road.

Is the campus near Ferozepur Road?

Yes. Township is approximately four kilometres from Ferozepur Road. Families travelling from areas served by Ferozepur Road can reach the campus by exiting onto the Township road network and following signs for Ali Road.

What classes does London School offer?

London School — Prof. Waris Mir Campus is a Cambridge Pathway Registered school offering Pre-Nursery through Class 7 in 2025–26. Class 8 launches in 2026–27 as the current Class 7 cohort progresses. The school does not currently offer IGCSE or O-Level.

Can I visit before applying?

Yes. We welcome campus visits Monday to Saturday, 8 AM to 4 PM. Book a tour through our admissions page or message us on WhatsApp at 0301-0499777, and we will confirm a time and brief you on parking arrangements before you arrive.
