**Common gaps in Tier 2:**
- Robotics/AI programs are claimed but not documented (no curriculum pages, no teacher bios, no student outcomes).
- Founder/legacy stories are absent or generic.
- Reviews and GBP profiles are inconsistent across branches.
- Slow mobile load times (most run heavy WordPress themes).
- No `llms.txt`, weak schema, no AI-search optimization.

## Tier 3 — Neighborhood-level (the real battleground)

These are smaller Cambridge/O-Level schools within ~5km of Ali Road, Ideal Park Township, Model Town, Garden Town, Township. They rank for "Cambridge school near [neighborhood]" because they have:
- A Google Business Profile with reviews
- A simple site with neighborhood mentions
- Local citations on Pakistani directory sites

**This is where we win.** A polished site + strong GBP + 60+ reviews + neighborhood landing pages will outrank most Tier 3 schools within 6–9 months.
